# User Manual (Phase 1)

1. **Log in** with your User ID and password (or your Windows/AD password if your account is an AD account). After 15 minutes idle you are logged out. Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes.
2. **First login:** you must set a new password (≥12 characters, upper+lower case, digit, symbol; not reused).
3. **Home** shows your dashboard cards. Cards for later modules appear greyed until those modules are installed.
4. **Reasons:** any change to controlled data asks for a *reason*. This is recorded with your name, role, date/time and IP in the audit trail.
5. **Electronic signature:** when an approval needs a signature you re-enter your password and state a reason/comment. The signature records your printed name, date/time, and meaning (e.g. *Approved by*), and is permanently linked to the record.
6. **Messages:** if something fails you will see a message and, for system faults, a reference such as `ERR-2026-000123` — quote it to the administrator.
7. You cannot approve a record you created, and you will see a clear explanation when a rule blocks you.
